SeeLater Privacy Policy

SeeLater is operated by Diwakar Raja ("SeeLater", "we", "us"). This policy explains what the SeeLater iPhone app and the seelater.app marketing website do with your information. The web version at web.seelater.app has a separate signed-in inbox. The native iPhone app stores its queue locally. If you separately connect the shared Shortcut to a web inbox, the Shortcut also sends supported links and sender details to that inbox, as described below.

The short version

  • The iPhone app needs no account and no sign-up.
  • The native app has no SeeLater analytics or advertising SDK. Embedded video services may collect technical and playback data under their own policies.
  • The native app stores saved links, sender details and watched progress on your iPhone. A separately connected web inbox stores its own copy of links and sender details.
  • The Shortcut may look up a sender in Contacts on your iPhone and pass the matched name with the link. You can also save a name manually.
  • Videos play in the official TikTok, YouTube and Instagram players, which load from those companies.

What the app stores, and where

SeeLater keeps a small library on your iPhone, inside the app's own storage. It includes:

  • Video links you save (TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts links).
  • Who sent each video, if known: the sender's phone number or email address as passed in by your Messages automation, or a name you type when you share or add a link.
  • An optional contact name for that sender (see Contacts below).
  • Your activity in the app: which videos you've watched, when each one was saved, and your settings (such as autoplay).

The native iPhone app does not upload this library to SeeLater. If you separately connect the Shortcut to SeeLater Web, the Shortcut sends supported links and sender details to your authenticated web inbox; that is a separate queue. Like other app data, it can be included in your own iPhone backups (iCloud or computer), under your Apple account settings.

SeeLater never downloads, copies or stores the videos themselves. It only stores links.

Automatic saving from Messages (optional)

If you choose to set it up, SeeLater uses an automation in Apple's Shortcuts app that you install and turn on. SeeLater can't read your Messages on its own.

  • The automation checks incoming messages that contain a web link, on your iPhone. If the message contains a supported TikTok, Instagram Reel or YouTube link, it passes only that link and the sender to SeeLater. Other messages, and the rest of the conversation text, are not passed to SeeLater.
  • SeeLater keeps the supported video link and the sender, and ignores everything else.
  • You can turn the automation off or delete it in the Shortcuts app at any time.

Share → SeeLater and Add link

When you share a link to SeeLater from another app, or paste one with Paste a video link, SeeLater saves the video link. Everything stays on your iPhone. (Shared and pasted videos appear under Unassigned, since there's no sender.)

Sender names

The shared SeeLater Shortcut can match the incoming sender to Contacts on your iPhone and pass the matched name, phone number or email with a supported video link. It does not upload your whole address book. Apple controls Shortcuts permissions and may ask for access. SeeLater also lets you enter or edit a sender name manually in Settings. The native app does not request its own Contacts permission. A web connection sends the matched sender details to your private web inbox.

Connecting SeeLater Web

Web sign-in and connection are optional and separate from using the native app. When connected, our Cloudflare-hosted service stores supported video links, sender identifiers and available names, save dates, watched state and preferences for your account. Google sign-in or email-code sign-in supplies your account identifier, email and available profile name. Resend delivers email sign-in codes. The Shortcut excludes surrounding conversation text and unrelated links. Native and web watched progress do not sync. You can delete web saves and manage or revoke Shortcut connections in the web app. Contact support@seelater.app to request deletion of your web account and associated stored data. We do not sell this information or use it for targeted advertising.

Video players from TikTok, YouTube and Instagram

To play a video, SeeLater opens that platform's official embedded player inside the app:

  • TikTok: TikTok's embed player
  • YouTube: YouTube's privacy-enhanced embed (youtube-nocookie.com)
  • Instagram: Instagram's embed

These players load directly from TikTok, Google/YouTube and Instagram/Meta. Like any web page, they receive standard technical information from your iPhone (such as your IP address, device and browser details, and which video is being played), and they may use cookies or similar storage inside the player. Their own privacy policies cover what they collect:

  • TikTok: https://www.tiktok.com/legal/page/us/privacy-policy/en (outside the US: https://www.tiktok.com/legal/privacy-policy)
  • YouTube / Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Instagram / Meta: https://privacycenter.instagram.com/policy

We don't receive any information from these players. If you tap "Open in TikTok/Instagram/YouTube," the video opens in that app or website, under its own policy.

Some shared links are short links (for example a TikTok short link). To find the actual video, SeeLater may send a simple request to that same link on the platform's site, the way a browser would. That request goes to the platform, not to us.

Apple

You download SeeLater from the App Store, and automatic saving runs in Apple's Shortcuts app. Apple's privacy policy applies to those: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

The seelater.app website

The seelater.app marketing website is a simple information page about the iPhone app. It has no sign-up form, and we don't sell or share visitor information. The connected web inbox is described above.

This website uses no analytics, cookies, or trackers, and fonts are served from the site itself. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses for security and operations purposes, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply and help you.

Deleting your data

Data in the iPhone app is on your iPhone, so you're in control of it:

  • Remove one video: tap the trash icon under the video and confirm Delete video.
  • Automatic limit: SeeLater keeps the newest 100 videos by default (you can change this in Settings). When the limit is reached, the oldest saves are removed.
  • Delete everything: use Settings → Delete all SeeLater data, or delete the app. The in-app action removes saved links, sender names, watched progress and setup state. Copies in older iPhone backups are kept or deleted under your backup settings.
  • Stop automatic saving: turn off or delete the "Save to SeeLater" automation in the Shortcuts app.
  • Shortcut access: manage the Shortcut and its permissions in Shortcuts. Deleting native data does not delete a separately connected web inbox.

The native app does not send us a copy of its library. A connected web inbox is separate: removing the native app or its data does not remove web data; contact us for web account deletion. If you've emailed us and want that conversation deleted, ask at support@seelater.app.

Children

The iPhone app is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children through the app. Because the iPhone app doesn't send your data to us, we don't receive app data from any user, including children. If you believe a child has sent us information by email, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how SeeLater handles information, we'll update this page and the effective date above. If a change is significant, for example if the app ever starts sending data to a server, we'll explain it in the app or the App Store release notes before it takes effect.
