Privacy: what gets saved

The shortcut filters matching messages on your iPhone. It passes only supported video links and sender details to SeeLater. Conversation text and unrelated links are excluded. SeeLater saves links, sender details and watch progress. Automatic saving currently supports Messages only.

It saves supported video links from incoming messages. Then open SeeLater to watch them.

Optional: quiet Shortcuts notifications

Shortcuts may show an alert each time a video is saved. If these get annoying, you can hide the alerts. Keep Allow Notifications on for automatic saving.

1

Open Settings → Screen Time.

2

Tap See All App & Website Activity. Choose Week and scroll down to Notifications.

3

Under Notifications, tap Show More until you see Shortcuts.

4

Tap Shortcuts.

Can’t tap Shortcuts? Use the arrows at the top to switch to last week, then back to this week. Try Shortcuts again.

5

Leave Allow Notifications on. Keep the switch green.

6

Turn off all three alert types: Lock Screen, Notification Center, and Banners. All three circles should be empty.

7

Turn Sounds and Show on Mac off. Leave Announce Notifications off too.

This silences alerts for all your shortcuts, not just SeeLater. Allow Notifications stays on. You can restore the alerts from the same screen. These steps may vary with your iOS version.
