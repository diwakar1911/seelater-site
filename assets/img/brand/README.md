# Third-party brand icons (unaltered official files)

These files were downloaded from each company’s official brand / press / developer
brand-resource pages on 15 November 2026. They are copied unaltered. SeeLater is
not affiliated with TikTok, Instagram, Meta, YouTube or Google. The marks are
used only as nominative source labels on the landing-page graphic, with required
clear space, and with a trademark notice on the page.

Do not recolor, restyle, crop, rotate, or add shadows or outlines to these files.

## TikTok

- Official source: TikTok for Developers, Design Guidelines
  https://developers.tiktok.com/doc/getting-started-design-guidelines/
- Asset pack (linked from that page as “TikTok’s Logo and Button packs”):
  https://sf16-va.tiktokcdn.com/obj/eden-va2/uvzhqeh7nuhd/tt4d/logo-pack.zip
- File used: `Dev Portal Logo Pack/TikTok Logo Pack/TikTok – Icons/TikTok_Icon_Black_Square.png`
- Saved here as: `tiktok/TikTok_Icon_Black_Square.png`
- Brand / legal guidelines linked from the same page:
  https://tiktokbrandbook.com/d/HhXfjVK1Poj9/legal

## Instagram

- Official source: Meta Brand Resource Center, Instagram brand assets and guidelines
  https://www.meta.com/brand/resources/instagram/instagram-brand/
- Asset pack linked from that page: `IG_brand_asset_pack_2023.zip`
- Files used (official static glyphs, unaltered):
  - `01 Static Glyph/03 Black Glyph/Instagram_Glyph_Black.svg`
    → `instagram/Instagram_Glyph_Black.svg` (light backgrounds)
  - `01 Static Glyph/02 White Glyph/Instagram_Glyph_White.svg`
    → `instagram/Instagram_Glyph_White.svg` (dark backgrounds / the SeeLater feed)

## YouTube (Shorts)

- Official source: YouTube Brand Resources
  https://www.youtube.com/howyoutubeworks/resources/brand-resources/
  (canonical site: https://brand.youtube/)
- Icon file used (official Google product logo, unaltered SVG):
  https://www.gstatic.com/images/branding/productlogos/youtube/v9/192px.svg
  → `youtube/youtube_productlogo_v9_192px.svg`
- Clear space and minimum size follow YouTube’s published icon rules on that
  page: clear space at least the size of the triangle in the icon; minimum
  20 dp / 20 px where the layout allows.
- A separate official “YouTube Shorts” logomark was not offered as a download
  on YouTube Brand Resources or Google’s product-logo CDN on 15 November 2026.
  The official YouTube icon is therefore used next to the words “YouTube Shorts”.
