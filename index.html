A few things worth knowing

Questions, answered plainly.

Does this leave my phone?
SeeLater keeps your queue on your device. We don't need a cloud account to show you what you were sent.
Which apps does it work with?
TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts to start. The links themselves can come from Messages or any other chat you already use.
What does it cost?
The price will be on the App Store listing. We won't invent one here, and nothing is charged from this page.
Which phones does it work on?
iPhone, for now. SeeLater is iOS-only.
