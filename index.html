How it works

They send links. You get a feed.

You don't have to talk anyone into a new app. They keep sending links the way they already do.

  1. 01

    They send you links

    In Messages, or wherever you already talk. TikToks, Reels, Shorts. The usual pile.

  2. 02

    SeeLater collects them

    Those links land in one place, instead of getting buried in the thread.

  3. 03

    You scroll the lot

    One vertical feed, with watched and unwatched marked, so you can actually finish it.

A few things worth knowing

Questions, answered plainly.

Does this leave my device?
The iPhone app keeps your queue on your iPhone. It needs no account. The web version needs a sign-in.
Which apps does it work with?
TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts to start. The links themselves can come from Messages or any other chat you already use.
What does it cost?
The price will be on the App Store listing. We won't invent one here, and nothing is charged from this page.
Where can I use it?
The iPhone app is iOS-only. The web version is for iPhone and needs a sign-in — open it at web.seelater.app in Safari, and you can add it to your Home Screen. The Save to SeeLater Shortcut is set up on iPhone.
How do I set up the Shortcut?
The Save to SeeLater Shortcut runs on iPhone. It saves TikTok, Reels, and Shorts links from Messages in the background. See the setup walkthrough.
