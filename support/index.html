FAQ

How do I set up automatic saving from Messages?

SeeLater can save TikTok, Instagram Reel and YouTube links people text you, automatically, using a one-time automation in Apple's Shortcuts app. The app walks you through it with pictures. There's also a full walkthrough at Set up the Shortcut.

  1. Open SeeLater and tap Set up automatic saving.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Install Shortcut, then tap Add Shortcut.
  3. In Shortcuts, find the Save to SeeLater tile. Touch and hold it, then choose Edit Shortcut.
  4. Tap Edit, expand the arrow next to the message trigger, and turn on the Automation.
  5. Make sure it runs without asking: Run Immediately on, or Confirm Before Run off, depending on your iOS version.
  6. Test it: when someone texts you a TikTok, Reel or Shorts link, it appears in SeeLater.

The automation checks messages that contain a web link, on your iPhone, and passes only supported video links (and the sender) to SeeLater. Other links and the rest of the conversation are never passed to SeeLater. Because it checks any message with a link, Shortcuts may show a notification even when nothing was saved (see "quiet the Shortcuts notifications" below). Menu names can differ slightly between iOS versions; the in-app guide shows the current screens.

How do I save a video with Share → SeeLater?

In TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Messages, Safari or almost any app, tap Share on the video or link, then choose SeeLater and tap Save. The video appears the next time you open SeeLater, under Unassigned.

  • Instagram: tap the paper-plane (Share) button, then Share to… (or the share/more option), then SeeLater.
  • TikTok: tap Share, then More or Other, then SeeLater.

Can I add a link by hand, or try the app first?

Yes. Tap Paste a video link (on the empty feed, or in Settings → Add videos), paste one or more TikTok, Instagram Reel or YouTube links, and tap Save. No Shortcut setup is needed. Pasted videos appear under Unassigned.

Why won't a video play?

SeeLater plays videos in each platform's official embedded player and never downloads them. A video may not play if:

  • it's private, deleted, age-restricted or region-restricted,
  • the creator has turned off embedding,
  • you're offline or the connection is slow, or
  • the platform is temporarily limiting its player.

Tap Try again, or tap Open in TikTok / Instagram / YouTube to watch it there. Instagram's player in particular can be inconsistent with some Reels.

How do I quiet the Shortcuts notifications?

iOS may show a small Shortcuts notification each time a video is saved. To hide these alerts, open SeeLater → Settings → Optional: quiet Shortcuts notifications, which shows each step with pictures. In short: open the iPhone Settings app, find Shortcuts in your notification activity list (tap Show More until it appears), and turn off the alert styles you don't want. Keep "Allow Notifications" on for Shortcuts, or automatic saving may stop working.

How do I delete my data?

Everything the iPhone app saves stays on your iPhone. To remove a single video, tap the trash icon under it and confirm Delete video. To erase everything, delete the SeeLater app. To stop automatic saving, turn off or delete the Save to SeeLater automation in Shortcuts. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Troubleshooting

Automatic saving doesn't work

  • Is the automation on? Open Shortcuts → Automation and check that the Save to SeeLater automation is turned on. New automations import switched off.
  • Is Run Immediately on? If the automation is set to ask first ("Run After Confirmation" or Confirm Before Run on), iOS waits for you to tap a notification before anything is saved. Switch it to run immediately.
  • Are Shortcuts notifications allowed? Settings → Notifications → Shortcuts → Allow Notifications must stay on.
  • Does the message contain a supported link? Supported: TikTok video links (including short links), Instagram Reel links, and YouTube Shorts, youtu.be and youtube.com/watch links. Instagram photo posts, profiles and links shortened by other services aren't saved. Use Share → SeeLater or Paste a video link for those.
  • Did you reinstall the shortcut? If you reinstalled the shortcut or SeeLater, delete the old automation and set it up again from Set up automatic saving.
  • Is the shortcut up to date? Install the latest Save to SeeLater shortcut from inside the app.

SeeLater isn't in the share sheet

  • Swipe the row of app icons in the share sheet to the end, tap More, and turn on (or favorite) SeeLater.
  • Open SeeLater once after installing or updating. iOS sometimes needs that before the share option appears.
  • Some apps only show their own share options. Use Copy link there, then use Paste a video link in SeeLater.
  • Still missing? Restart your iPhone and try again.

A link was saved twice

SeeLater skips duplicates from the same sender. The same video from two different people shows up once in each person's queue, so you know both sent it. A video saved by Share or Paste lands in Unassigned, so it can also appear in the sender's queue if the automation saved it too. Delete the extra one with the trash icon, and email us if it keeps happening.

The sender shows as a number, or as unknown

  • The Messages automation passes the sender's phone number or email. To see names instead, allow Contacts access when SeeLater asks, or later in Settings → Privacy & Security → Contacts. Matching happens on your iPhone, and the number is shown if there's no clear match.
  • Videos saved with Share or Paste a video link don't have a sender, so they appear under Unassigned.
